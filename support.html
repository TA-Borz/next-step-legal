Support

For questions about Next Step, contact turpal-ali@web.de.

Before contacting support

  • Restart the app.
  • Check available device storage.
  • Use Settings to change language or clear local data.

Include in your message

  • App version.
  • Device model and operating-system version.
  • A short description of what happened.

Do not send passwords, identity documents, or financial information.
