Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Next Step works offline and does not require account registration. The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, external APIs, or AI services and does not send user data to a server.

Local data

Language, country, questionnaire answers, route selection, and progress may be stored locally on the device. This data is used only to provide recommendations and display progress.

Sharing and sale

Next Step does not sell, rent, share, transmit, or upload personal data.

Deletion

Local data can be deleted in the app settings or by uninstalling the app.

Career disclaimer

The app provides educational guidance and does not guarantee employment, admission, interviews, job offers, or a specific income.
